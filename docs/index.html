What the data shows

From the analysis notebook, data through September 28, 2026.

  • Potholes depend on where you live. In 2025 the 8 slowest community areas, on the West and Northwest Sides, waited a median of 43 days for a pothole request to close, vs 12 days in the rest of the city (3.6x). The same areas were slower in every year since 2019.
  • Pothole waits rose 79% in 2025 citywide, to a median of 13.5 days.
  • Street-light requests close 11.5x faster than in 2019: a median of 2.4 days in 2025, down from 27.6.
  • Lower-income areas do not wait longer for any of the five services. Rat complaints close faster there.
  • Cleaning matters. Keeping the city's own logged work would make 2024-25 pothole waits look 43% shorter.
